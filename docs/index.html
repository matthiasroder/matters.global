matters.global

matters.global is the layer between human intention and AI execution, turning goals into a dependency map so AI can act on what can actually move forward.

Start here Understand the idea
What

matters.global is software that turns human goals into an execution-ready dependency map.

It captures goals, blockers, and conditions, then maps their dependencies so you can see exactly what must happen before progress is possible.

Humans define intent and outcomes; AI agents help extract structure, recommend the next steps, and execute actions. For example, in science they can research the next paper, while in business they can draft a progress report.

About

A Sonophilia Foundation project for creative execution.

matters.global is part of the Sonophilia Foundation, a non-profit advancing and sharing creativity research through collaboration between science, business, education, and technology.

Creativity is the human capacity to solve unsolved matters in a new and useful way. We use that capacity to keep the focus on what is truly solvable, what is unknown, and what should happen next.

It was created as a Sonophilia Foundation initiative by co-founders Seda and Matthias Röder to build an interconnected map of all known problems. The project was started in 2020 and went through several phases with individuals from the Sonophilia Foundation, Ocean Protocol, and the Foresight Institute.
